## What we do
AI implementation: find a real, repetitive business problem, design the automation,
build it, test it, and prove it works.

## Voice
- Plain English. Define jargon the first time it appears.
- Concrete over impressive. Show the thing working.
- Never talk down to the reader.

## Claims we are allowed to make
Only what has evidence in a `projects/<project>/EVIDENCE/` report. Every public claim
maps to one of:

| Level | Means | May we say "it works"? |
|---|---|---|
| concept | an idea, nothing built | no |
| prototype | something runs, untested | no |
| tested MVP | acceptance criteria verified | yes, with scope stated |
| deployed | live, in use | yes |
| client deployment | live for a paying client | yes, with permission |
| verified result | measured outcome for a client | yes, with the number and permission |

## Claims we never make
- A client result without that client's permission.
- A capability we have not built at least once.
- A metric we cannot show the source of.
